Actual continuous-phase Callisto startup stress at the three preselected scales. Each upper panel shows the unsmoothed held physical frequency of all nodes. Each lower shaded band spans the minimum and maximum over all six directed buffers and every integer crossing within each 1000-reference-tick display bin; per-edge bin extrema are in the CSV. Thus the plot preserves sampled-bin extrema rather than implying that the shaded interior is continuously occupied. Dashed lines mark the target. Gates and saturation are reported independently in the audit; these cases are finite-time startup tests, not post-centering disturbances or reference-model certificates.
